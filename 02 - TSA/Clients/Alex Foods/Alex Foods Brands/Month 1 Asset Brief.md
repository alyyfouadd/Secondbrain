# Month 1 Asset Brief — Alex Foods

**The twenty Package A month-1 assets, specified to the point a supplier can build one without asking a question.** This is the note [[Package A Month 1]] §3 calls *"the single highest-leverage thing to make before month 1 starts"* — the brief is the deliverable, because Aly directs and two suppliers execute.

**What owns what.** Ideas and pillar weighting are [[Content Plan]] §5 and [[Post Archetypes]] §3 — this note does not re-argue them. Colour values are [[Colour System]]. Tone and the never-say list are [[Brand Voice Guide]]. Campaign facts are [[Giveaway Programme]]. Delivery state is [[Delivery Register]]. **This note owns the production spec and the month-1 posting calendar** — the twenty assets, what each one is, and the day each one goes out. **Where its allocation differs from [[Content Plan]] §5, this note is live and §5 is superseded**; the reasoning is in §5 below.

**Month 1 IS the school campaign** ([[Decisions]] #26), and **the campaign go-live date is D0.** Every deadline below is relative to it, because the date does not exist yet.

## 1. The standing spec — applies to all twenty, stated once

**A supplier reads this section once and never asks about it again.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Feed** | **1080 × 1350**, 4:5. The default. Every graphic unless the row says otherwise |
| **Story / Reel / TikTok** | **1080 × 1920**, 9:16 |
| **Safe area** | **8% margin all round.** Nothing important inside it. On 9:16 keep the **top 250 px and bottom 350 px** clear of type — that is where the platform puts its own furniture |
| **Motion length** | Animated still **3–6 s**, seamless loop. Animation **12–20 s**, hard cut, no loop |
| **Motion delivery** | **MP4, H.264, 1080 × 1920 and a 1080 × 1350 crop of the same cut.** Plus the final frame as a PNG — it becomes a free story slot |
| **Graphic delivery** | **PNG, sRGB, no compression pass.** Layered working file alongside it |
| **Type** | Plex Sans Arabic / IBM Plex Sans per [[Type System]]. **Arabic is written first and English is fitted to it**, never the reverse |
| **Type colour on a flavour field** | **Ink `#141414` is the default and white is the exception.** White is correct on **only four fields**: BeBo Cola, AlRawy Cocktail, AlRawy Peach, POLEKA Deep Purple. **Bright Pink `#EC008C` always carries a dark keyline** — both options sit near 4.2:1 and neither is comfortable ([[Colour System]] §8) |
| **Field colours** | **Named, never re-typed as a value.** Every field name below resolves in [[Colour System]] §5, which owns the hex — **if it is not in that table it is not in the system** |
| **The seal** | `logos-vector/alex-seal-flat.svg`, **never the raster cut-out.** Foot of the frame, on Paper or white. **Never on a navy ground** — it reads 1.43:1 there and the ring text disappears |
| **Never enlarge a mark past its source resolution** | The four range marks are raster. Scaling one up is the defect the recipe already names |

**Filename convention — agreed once, used for three months:**

```
AF-M1-<code>-<range>-<subject>-<ratio>-v<n>.<ext>
AF-M1-G04-bebo-apple-4x5-v1.png
AF-M1-A02-bebo-mechanic-9x16-v2.mp4
```

**Codes are `A01`–`A02` animations, `S01`–`S06` animated stills, `G01`–`G12` graphics**, exactly as numbered below. **A supplier never invents a name.**

**Every asset obeys [[Brand Voice Guide]] §7 without exception:** no health claim, no «طبيعي ١٠٠٪», no nutritional number, no competitor comparison, no price superlative, no named shop, no company-age claim.

> **One open question sits over every line of BeBo copy below.** The pack's own legal declaration is **«مشروب سكري صناعي»**, not «عصير» ([[Decisions]] #30). **No BeBo line in this brief uses the word «عصير»** — every one of them survives either answer. **Do not let one in during revisions.**

## 2. The calendar — what posts, when, where

**This is Package A's contracted "content calendar", and it is the thing the spec below could not do on its own.** A spec says what an asset is; a calendar says when it earns its money.

**D0 is the day the school campaign goes live.** Everything is relative to it because the date does not exist yet — **the moment the client gives the term dates, this whole table resolves to real days.**

**Rhythm: Facebook 4 a week, TikTok 2, Instagram mirrors Facebook.** Derived from twenty assets across 4.35 weeks, **not from ambition** — and every asset cross-posts, which is the only way twenty originals cover the slots ([[Content Plan]] §3).

> **Eight of the twenty are the giveaway** — four winner posts, three mechanic assets, the prize. That is the month pointed at the client's own number-one priority rather than spread politely across four ranges.

> ### The rebalance, and why the two source notes were not simply obeyed
>
> **[[Content Plan]] §5 and [[Post Archetypes]] §3 allocate the same twenty assets two different ways, and nobody had put them side by side.** §5's list gives the winner post **one** slot. §3's weighting gives it **four**, at ~35% — the largest single share of the month.
>
> **§3 is right and §5 was written first.** Three reasons, all of them the playbook's own:
>
> 1. **Proof beats every claim a food brand is allowed to make.** A real person holding a real prize outside a real shop answers *"is this giveaway actually real?"* — the question stopping people entering. Nothing TSA is legally allowed to write competes with it.
> 2. **It is free.** The client has been shooting winners since August. **It is the only content in the month that costs nothing to originate**, in a month where eight assets ride on an animator nobody has hired.
> 3. **The contracted KPI is comments and views**, and winner posts are the highest-engagement thing this account owns, by a distance.
>
> **What moved:** S02 *(was a second BeBo product still, justified only by "it proves the template repeats" — a production argument, not an audience one)*, G05 *(BeBo cola)* and G09 *(a static twin of A02 — the month does not need a still **and** a film of the same idea)*. **G04 became the third mechanic asset** instead of a fourth BeBo product shot.
>
> **The risk, named out loud: four of twenty now ride on winner media and written permission, and neither has arrived.** **If that permission never comes, three product graphics come straight back off the bench** — S02→BeBo mango, G05→BeBo cola, G09→the school-morning still. **That fallback is the reason this rebalance is safe to make. Do not lose it.**

## 6. Who makes what, and what it costs

**Rates agreed 20 September 2026** ([[Decisions]] #31). Full books in [[TSA Money]] — **that note owns the arithmetic, this one owns the split.**

| Who | Assets | Rate |
|---|---|---|
| **Mahmoud** | **The 12 graphics** | **300 each = 3,600/month** |
| **Mahmoud** | The separate visual kit and guidelines | **2,000, one-off**, against the Foundation fee not the retainer |
| **CGI animator** | **2 animations + 6 animated stills** | **Unfilled. Unpriced. Eight of twenty assets** |
| **Media buyer** | Paid campaign management | 2,000/month |
| **TSA** | Direction, briefs, copy, posting, comments | — |

### The proposal worth putting to Mahmoud before hiring an animator

**At 300 a post he is cheap, and an animated still is a designed frame with a small motion pass on top.** Split it: **Mahmoud designs the six still frames at his post rate (1,800), and the animator only does the motion pass.** That drops the animator's scope from **eight originated pieces to two films plus six passes on finished art** — which should cut his price materially, and it de-risks the single largest hole in the month. **Worth one message before the role gets filled at a panic rate.**

## 8. Supplier terms still unagreed, and all four get expensive in week three

1. **Who owns the working files.** Clause 5 makes them TSA property in the client contract — **that has to be agreed with Mahmoud, not just written about him.**
2. **How many revision rounds he gets.** The client gets one. **If Mahmoud gets unlimited, the margin is wherever Mahmoud stops.**
3. **Whether 300 is per asset or per delivered post.** A feed post and its story crop are two files and one asset. **Say which before the first invoice.**
4. **The handoff route.** Twenty assets a month, three months, two suppliers, one poster.
