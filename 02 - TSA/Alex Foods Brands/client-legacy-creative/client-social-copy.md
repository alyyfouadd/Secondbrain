## What this overturns

### 1. POLEKA is a fruit-flavoured DRINK, in the client's own words

**«بوليكا مشروب بطعم الفاكهة».** Not a jelly, not a candy. **And it is the exact construction printed on the BeBo packs** — «مشروب بطعم الخوخ». **The client files POLEKA in the same category as BeBo.**

**That is the fourth correction to this product in one night**, and it is the only one from a primary source.

### 2. «لوليتا» is the category word, and TSA had never heard of it

**«هتسميها لوليتا، عصير، مشروب، براحتك»** — *call it lolita, juice, a drink, whatever you like.*

**لوليتا is what people in the market actually call this format.** The client uses it as a generic, and even brands with it: **«لوليتا تومان»**. **This word appears nowhere in the vault**, and it is the word the customer already has in their head.

### 3. 2MAN's real marketing is NOSTALGIA, aimed at adults

**«العمر مجرد رقم»** — *age is just a number.* **«انتعاش بحكايات زمان»** — *refreshment with stories from the old days.*

**The vault has 2MAN as "talking to the kid, the day is yours, go outside."** That was read off the running-boy character. **Their own copy is aimed at an adult remembering their childhood**, and «عيش جو المغامرة» sits on a pack while the social voice does something completely different.

### 4. انتعاش is confirmed as their word

**Used twice in one post.** Aly's instruction to prefer it over برد was not a stylistic preference, **it was him echoing the client's actual register**, and the POLEKA line is stronger for it than the reasoning that produced it.

### 5. They publish the price, and it is 2 EGP

**«و ب ٢ جنيه بس».** **The first real price datum TSA holds**, and it comes from the client's own published copy rather than from an answer.

**It also matters for the never-say list.** [[Brand Voice Guide]] §7 bans *price claims* — «الأرخص», unverifiable superlatives. **It does not ban stating a price the client themselves publishes**, and the distinction should be written down before someone reads the rule too broadly and strips a real selling point out of the copy.

### 6. The tone is cheekier and more emoji-heavy than the guide allows

**«عذرا ايها السيدات و الساده الكرام، بس احنا جايين نعمل بوليكا»** — a formal address collapsing into a joke. **That is a real comic device and the guide has nothing like it.**

**Emoji: 💣💣 ❤️❤️ 🎉😁 👌.** Doubled, mid-post and at the end. §6 caps POLEKA at three and forbids strings of the same one. **The client breaks that rule in their own copy.**

### 7. Scarcity is already part of their playbook

**«عشان الكمية محدودة»** — *because quantities are limited.* Urgency is a campaign lever the guide reserves for the giveaway. **They already use it on ordinary posts.**

---

## What TSA should do with it

**Not copy it.** Their copy has real problems: it is long, it mixes registers, «بطعم ولا في الخيال» is a claim, and the doubled emoji read as loud rather than warm.

**But it is the ground truth for register**, and three things in it are better than what the guide currently says: **انتعاش**, **لوليتا**, and the **formal-to-casual comic flip**. Those go in.

> **The correction that matters most: the guide was written from packaging and this is the client speaking.** Wherever the two disagree, **this wins**, and [[Brand Voice Guide]] needs a pass against it rather than a patch.
