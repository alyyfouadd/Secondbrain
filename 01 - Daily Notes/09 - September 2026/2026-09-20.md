## Session 1 — 3:20 AM: Their lines were the answer

Aly, on the ten rejected slogans: **"their slogans were good."**

### That is a better diagnosis than mine was

Yesterday I blamed rhythm, rhyme and idiom. **The real problem was doctrine.**

Their own in-market lines are «طعم أحلى مع أليكس فودز», «عيش جو المغامرة» and «جودة نثق بها منذ ٢٠ عامًا». **Every one of them names the brand or sits right next to the product.** Warm, direct, everyday comparatives, no cleverness.

**And §1 of [[Slogans and Song]] tells you never to do that.** *Never describe the product, never name it, sell the feeling.* **That rule is correct for a headline in a funnel and it is wrong for this brand in this market**, and I applied it to ten lines without ever checking it against the client's own working creative, which was sitting in `client-legacy-creative/` the whole time.

The note even warned that headline rules do not transfer wholesale to slogans. **It named specificity and urgency as the things that break. It missed the one that actually broke.**

### The consequence nobody had drawn

**If their lines are good, the master line already exists.** «طعم أحلى مع أليكس فودز» is in market and working.

**The same rule that protects عيش جو المغامرة protects it: replacing a working line to justify a deliverable destroys equity TSA was hired to build.** So two of the five brands are already solved, and the honest deliverable is **three lines and a giveaway, not five and a giveaway.** Smaller bill of work, better answer, and it only appeared once the register question was settled.

### The rewrite

Built on their patterns rather than against them. **مع + أحلى** is their structure, so BeBo gets «مع بيبو، البيت أحلى» and POLEKA gets «مع بوليكا، اللعب أحلى». AlRawy borrows their own trust word: **«الراوي.. طعم تثق فيه»** echoes «جودة نثق بها», which is the client's own language for exactly what AlRawy is for.

**All marked drafts for Aly's ear.** Ten lines passed every structural test yesterday and failed the only test that counts.

## Session 4 — 4:10 AM: BeBo has a line, and Aly out-picked me

**«بيبو معاك كل يوم».** He took the third alternate, not the one I led with.

### He was right, and the reason is structural rather than taste

I recommended **«مع بيبو، الفسحة أحلى»** because الفسحة is the child's own word and it ties to the one occasion the client named. **That is exactly what is wrong with it.**

**It locks the brand to school.** BeBo sells all year, and school stops for three months. **An occasion-bound line goes quiet with the occasion**, which makes it a campaign line wearing a brand line's clothes. §4's own test 3 says a brand line has to hold across every context, and I recommended one that does not.

**«كل يوم» does the opposite: it claims the habit.** For an everyday affordable drink that is the whole ask, and it is the sort of claim a brand line can hold for years without ever needing a document behind it.

**The rest of it checks out too:** «معاك» is second person singular and direct, so there is no parent in between, which was the correction being made an hour earlier. It names no format, so it survives the back-of-pack answer either way. It works across all five SKUs. Brand name inside the line, in their register, no cleverness. And three beats — بي-بو · مَ-عاك · كل-يوم — so a child can repeat it.

### One territory line that fell out of it

**BeBo owns «معاك». POLEKA owns «صاحبك».** Accompaniment against companionship, and they sit close enough that if both ranges use both words the two brands blur. **POLEKA's entire mechanic is the character as a friend**, so «صاحبك» came out of BeBo's words-in list in the same pass.

### Where the deliverable stands

**Three of five settled.** The master keeps «طعم أحلى مع أليكس فودز» and 2MAN keeps «عيش جو المغامرة» — both theirs, both in market, both working, and neither touched. BeBo now has a line. **Outstanding: AlRawy, POLEKA, and the giveaway song.**

**Five lines, never more.** Date and countdown · today's item from the plan · what actually moved · what is blocking and **who by name** · money inside seven days. Then one line naming the single most useful thing to do first.

### What the render caught

**The overflow probe was broken and reporting a catastrophe.** It skipped the footer by testing `className`, but the footer is a `<footer>` **tag** with no class, so every one of the 39 pages reported overflowing at a uniform 1089px. **A real 1115px overflow was hiding inside that noise and would have shipped.** Rule now banked: *every page failing by the same number is a bug in the probe, never in the document.*

**Then the probe's fix pointed at the wrong section.** `render()` sorts by section id, so walking `SEC` in insertion order said "page 28 is section 12" — a section with nothing wrong with it. **The page-to-section map has to be read out of the generated HTML.** Caught by opening the page and reading its header instead of trusting the map.
