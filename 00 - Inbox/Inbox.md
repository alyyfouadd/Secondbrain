# Inbox

**The front door. Everything unsorted lands here first, and nothing stays.**

Capture beats filing. A thought that has to find its home before it can be written down is a thought that stays in my head or dies in a chat scrollback. So it lands here, unfiled, and gets routed later — but it does get routed.

## Where things go from here

Every item in this folder is in transit. These are the exits:

| It's about | It goes to | Index |
|---|---|---|
| A client — their deal, their brand, their delivery | `02 - TSA/Clients/<Client>/` | [[Clients]] |
| The agency itself — offers, positioning, scaling, money | `02 - TSA/` | [[TSA]] · [[TSA Money]] |
| TSA's own identity | `02 - TSA/TSA Brand/` | [[TSA Brand]] |
| Life outside the agency — money, health, training, buying | `03 - Personal/` | [[Personal]] |
| Reference material any project can use, and Jobs | `05 - Resources/` | [[Resources]] |
| Finished and worth keeping | `04 - Archive/` | [[Archive]] |
| Something still open that needs doing, not filing | [[Active Priorities]] | — |

**A move is not finished until both indexes are updated in the same pass** — this one loses its entry, the destination gains one. That is the rule that stops this folder becoming a drawer.

## What does NOT come through here

- **A daily note.** [[Daily Notes]] is the append-only log of what happened. This folder is unsorted material that still needs a home. A session's record is not unfiled, it is filed as a log.
- **Anything with an obvious home.** If it plainly belongs in an existing note, it goes straight there. The Inbox is for things that need a decision, not a detour on the way to a decision already made.
