# Giveaway Programme — Alex Foods

**How the client's giveaways actually work.** From Hussein via Aly, 20 September 2026. **None of this was in the vault**, and TSA had spent a session designing a mechanic the client does not use.

> **The correction that matters: TSA does not design this programme. It already exists, it is built into the packaging, and it is running right now.** TSA's job is to run it better.

---

## Two mechanics, two audiences, both already in the pack

| Who wins | How | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **تجار التجزئة · retailers** | **A gift coupon inside the carton** | **Printed into the carton**, not the pack |
| **المستهلك · the consumer** | **Scanning a QR code on the back of the pack** | **On the back of every باكو** |

**Both mechanics are physical and already shipped.** Nobody is adding a step, changing artwork or printing anything new. **TSA inherits a working machine.**

> **This is why the photograph-the-pack mechanic TSA proposed was wrong in a way worth remembering.** It was a reasonable design for a client with no mechanic. **This client has two, aimed at two different buyers, and has had them long enough to have winners.**

### What each mechanic implies for production

- **The QR code is on the back of the pack, so the back of the pack is now a hero surface.** Product imagery cannot only show the front. **§16 of the book assumes front-facing packshots throughout and needs a back-of-pack frame with a legible QR.**
- **The retailer coupon is in the carton, so trade content shows the carton.** Every visual asset TSA holds is a single pack on a flavour field. **There is no carton in the library**, and trade content cannot be made without one.

## Three campaigns, and the client has already ranked them

**Hussein's own order, 20 September:**

### 1 · هدايا المدارس — school gifts · **RUNNING NOW, AND THE PRIORITY**

**Prizes: كراسات · نوتة · مقلمة · أقلام · مساطر** — exercise books, a notebook, a pencil case, pens, rulers.

> **«ودي أهم حاجة حالياً علشان نلحق دخول المدارس»** — *the most important thing right now, to catch the start of school.*

**This is a race and the client says so.** Back-to-school in Egypt falls in late September, **which is this week or next**, and a school campaign that lands after the first day of term is a campaign that missed.

### 2 · هدايا العجلة والاسكوتر والكور — bicycle, scooter, balls · **RUNNING, LONG-TERM**

**Running for a long time and still running.** The client sends photos and videos of winners and will keep sending them. **This is the campaign the page screenshots showed** — «واحد اشترى بيبو... ورجع بعجلة».

**«وبعد ما نخلص هدايا المدارس هنكمل في الهدايا دي»** — it continues after the school campaign finishes.

### 3 · العمرة — the Umrah trade programme · **PAUSED**

**«دي هنسيبها حالياً لحد ما نخلص هدايا المدارس»** — parked until the school campaign is done.

**The annual trade loyalty programme**, winners chosen from **أعلى العملاء في حجم المسحوبات**, the highest-volume buyers across all company products. **The instrument that serves the client's stated goal of «mostly more shops», deliberately on hold.**

## The commercial problem this creates, and it needs Aly's decision

**The school campaign is the client's most urgent need and it is happening now. TSA's contracted work is a Brand Foundation due 9 October.**

**Package A month 1 has not started.** It starts on written Foundation approval, or on the arrival of usable product photography, whichever is later. **So content produced for the school campaign this week is work outside a paid month.**

**Three honest options:**

| Option | What it costs |
|---|---|
| **Decline politely and deliver the Foundation on time** | **Correct contractually and wrong commercially.** The client's most urgent need goes unanswered by the agency they just hired. |
| **Produce it as goodwill** | **A month's worth of urgency for free**, and it sets a precedent that urgent things happen outside the contract. |
| **Agree in writing that month 1 starts now** | **The honest one.** The client gets the campaign, TSA gets the month it is owed, and the Foundation still lands on 9 October. |

> **The third is the right answer and it has to be agreed before the work, not after.** *"We can start on the school campaign this week, and that starts month 1."* **One sentence, sent before anything is produced.**

## What TSA can actually add

**Not a mechanic. They have two.** What is missing is everything around them:

1. **Winner content as a format rather than a habit.** They post winner photos when they have them. **A fixed lockup, a fixed frame and a standing cadence turns that into a campaign.**
2. **The QR is invisible in their content.** Nobody scrolling knows the mechanic exists unless a post tells them. **The QR and the coupon need explaining, repeatedly, in the feed.**
3. **The trade programme has no consumer-facing story at all.** Retailers do not know it exists unless a rep tells them. **A carton, a coupon and a clear line about the Umrah draw is a trade asset the client does not have.**
4. **Nothing is timed.** School gifts are racing a deadline, the toys run forever, and Umrah is annual. **Three different clocks and no calendar.**
