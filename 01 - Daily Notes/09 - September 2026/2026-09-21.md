### Decisions Made

- **The pitch does not open with the website.** It opens with what their content is doing to the price of a bike they are trying to sell, and the site closes it as evidence. **Leading with the deliverable makes the meeting about the site's colours instead of their margin.**
- **The salesperson is a source before she is a door.** She holds every commercial fact the proposal needs and she is free. **And the marketing being replaced is hers — framed as taking the job she never asked for so she can sell, she becomes the champion in the room rather than the objection.**
- **Recommended the small yes first:** land the website this month, retainer in thirty days with a start date attached. **A business paying approximately nothing does not go to a monthly retainer in one meeting**, it goes quiet — and the site puts cash in this month.
- **Argued against padding the fee by 10,000 to fund the scooter.** It prices against Aly's need instead of their value, it arrives in the TSA pot rather than his pocket, and **it is the first thing that gets negotiated away because it is the part with no argument behind it.** Recommendation: full price in cash, scooter bought separately at a partner rate; a contra deal as the commercial fallback, because they pay in inventory at cost rather than at retail.
- **A new TSA pricing rule, and it is the first precedent that is not a single data point:** a month that includes a shoot never prices under a month that does not. `Alex Foods` pays 42,000 for twenty assets with no camera involved. **That is the floor to hold when the room pushes back.**
- **Named the thing nobody else would:** a scooter on installments is a fourth fixed monthly commitment against 23,000 of personal debt, an 18,000 tax with an unconfirmed date, and a cost of delivery that still has no total. **Not an argument against buying it — an argument for knowing the monthly number first.**
