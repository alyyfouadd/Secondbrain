## Session 11 — 3:51 AM: POLEKA is in, and the Colour and Type Kit is drafted

### What Got Done
- **Decision taken: POLEKA is inside the Foundation.** Four brands, no change to fee. Recorded across [[Alex Foods]], [[Alex Foods Brands]] and [[Active Priorities]], with the one remaining action being to get it in writing rather than to re-argue it.

- Set the architecture that the whole visual system hangs on: **three layers — parent (the Alex seal), brand (fixed logo colours), flavour (the field colour per SKU)** — and the rule that falls out of it, that the flavour colour owns the background while the brand colour owns the logo, and they never trade places. That single rule is what makes a shelf of five BeBo packs read as one brand.
- Caught a type problem that would have produced unusable work: **2MAN and POLEKA wordmarks are custom illustrated 3D lettering, not typefaces.** Neither can ever be retyped in a font. That is also the hard argument for why vector logo files are not an optional material.
- Wrote the bilingual type rules — Arabic set first and English fitted to it, never the reverse, because a layout designed in English breaks when Arabic is poured into it.

### What's Still In Progress
- The kit is **draft, not final**. Six things block sign-off: exact colour codes, font names, vector logos, which 2MAN design is live, what "Alex" actually is, and POLEKA in writing.
- 2MAN assets are hard-blocked until the client says which of the two designs is live.

### Decisions Made
- **POLEKA is in.** Aly's call, made knowingly with the cost stated.
- **2MAN's existing tagline عيش جو المغامرة is kept, not replaced.** It already sells the feeling rather than the product. Replacing a working line to justify a deliverable destroys brand equity TSA was hired to build.
- **The kit ships as a draft with provisional values rather than waiting for perfect data.** Eyeballed colours are good enough to design with and explicitly not good enough to print with, and the document says so on its face.
- **No single house palette.** Four brands with four distinct palettes is correct — it is what separates them on the same shelf — so the kit defines layers rather than flattening them.
