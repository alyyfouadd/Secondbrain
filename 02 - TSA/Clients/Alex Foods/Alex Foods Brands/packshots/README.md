## What these settled

1. **AlRawy is ready-to-drink, not a sachet to mix.** Aly corrected this on 19 Sep and the packs confirm it in print. **BeBo is the powder; AlRawy is the juice.** Any copy that implies AlRawy needs preparing is wrong.
2. **POLEKA's cola and strawberry really are the same pink**, visible side by side here. The ΔE 0.00 finding in [[Colour System]] §9 is confirmed against the client's own mockup.
3. **The trademark exposure is as bad as recorded.** The POLEKA cola bottle carries a contour bottle in red-and-white livery with "Cola Cola" in near-identical Spencerian script, and the 2MAN Bu:Zz cola stick shows a similar bottle. See [[Alex Foods Brands]].

**Still raster, still screenshots.** Fine for social and for this kit, which is screen-first. Not print artwork.

## Resolution warning, added 19 September 2026

**These are fine for a governance document and too small for a social asset where the pack is the hero.** Audited:

| | Size | 1080 × 1350 feed post? |
|---|---|---|
| `bebo-*.png` | **199 × 251** | **No** |
| `alrawy-*.png` | ~250 × 410 | No |
| `poleka-*.png` | ~130 × 520 | No |
| `2man-*.png` | ~830 × 175 | No |
| the range lineups above | ~1400–2600 wide | Yes, **as a strip**, not as a single pack |

They were sliced out of iPad screenshots, which was right at the time. **A BeBo sachet at 199 px tall blown up to fill a 1080-wide frame is mush.**

**The fix is not a shoot.** It is pack artwork at high resolution or vector, which the client can supply — they sent real Illustrator artwork of the master seal on 19 September, so the route is proven. **That is a separate ask from product photography** and conflating the two is why neither has arrived. See [[Package A Month 1]] §4.
