# Jobs

**One note per recurring agency job.** A Job hands a future session the complete skill for that job in one read: the procedure, the quality bar, the traps, and links to exactly the notes that job needs and nothing else.

This is the piece that turns the vault from a memory into an operating system. **Memory tells you what happened. A Job tells you how to do it again without paying the discovery tax twice.**

## The trigger

**The second time a task gets explained from scratch, it earns a Job note.** Not the first — the first time you are still finding out what the job is, and a runbook written then describes what was imagined rather than what happened.

## The shape

```
Jobs/
  <Job Name>/
    <Job Name>.md     <- the index: what this is, then the numbered steps
    <supporting notes>  <- one per step that needs more than a paragraph
```

Each step links to exactly what it needs. A session reads step one, opens what it points at, comes back, reads step two. By the last step the work is done.
