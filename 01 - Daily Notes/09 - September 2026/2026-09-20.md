## Session 1 — 3:20 AM: Their lines were the answer

Aly, on the ten rejected slogans: **"their slogans were good."**

### That is a better diagnosis than mine was

Yesterday I blamed rhythm, rhyme and idiom. **The real problem was doctrine.**

Their own in-market lines are «طعم أحلى مع أليكس فودز», «عيش جو المغامرة» and «جودة نثق بها منذ ٢٠ عامًا». **Every one of them names the brand or sits right next to the product.** Warm, direct, everyday comparatives, no cleverness.

**And §1 of [[Slogans and Song]] tells you never to do that.** *Never describe the product, never name it, sell the feeling.* **That rule is correct for a headline in a funnel and it is wrong for this brand in this market**, and I applied it to ten lines without ever checking it against the client's own working creative, which was sitting in `client-legacy-creative/` the whole time.

The note even warned that headline rules do not transfer wholesale to slogans. **It named specificity and urgency as the things that break. It missed the one that actually broke.**

### The consequence nobody had drawn

**If their lines are good, the master line already exists.** «طعم أحلى مع أليكس فودز» is in market and working.

**The same rule that protects عيش جو المغامرة protects it: replacing a working line to justify a deliverable destroys equity TSA was hired to build.** So two of the five brands are already solved, and the honest deliverable is **three lines and a giveaway, not five and a giveaway.** Smaller bill of work, better answer, and it only appeared once the register question was settled.

### The rewrite

Built on their patterns rather than against them. **مع + أحلى** is their structure, so BeBo gets «مع بيبو، البيت أحلى» and POLEKA gets «مع بوليكا، اللعب أحلى». AlRawy borrows their own trust word: **«الراوي.. طعم تثق فيه»** echoes «جودة نثق بها», which is the client's own language for exactly what AlRawy is for.

**All marked drafts for Aly's ear.** Ten lines passed every structural test yesterday and failed the only test that counts.

## Session 2 — 3:35 AM: The giveaway

### The mechanic decides everything else

**Photo of the pack, sent in, one winner a week.** Chosen against three alternatives, and the reasons are worth keeping:

- **Tag friends in comments** inflates reach and sells nothing. **The client wants shops, not tags.**
- **Code under the wrapper** needs a pack change and a print run. The packs are in market; outside scope.
- **Receipt upload** excludes every kiosk sale, which is most of them.

**Photo of the pack requires a purchase, produces content TSA can repost, and needs no pack change.** Weekly cadence gives the page a recurring reason to post and a deadline that renews instead of expiring.

### The song is a draft and says so

Brand name inside the hook, mechanic named in the second line, a metre that can be chanted **because children will perform it badly and that is the distribution.** Written, not produced: production is outside both stages.

**Marked draft deliberately.** A song is harder than a slogan, because it has to survive being sung badly.

### Decisions Made
- **Write in the client's register, not the playbook's.** The playbook is craft reference, not law, and **the client's own working creative outranks it on register.**
- **Two brands are solved and stay untouched.** The master line and 2MAN's tagline are theirs and they work.
- **Nothing rendered to PDF this session.** Unvalidated Arabic does not go into a build after yesterday.

## Session 3 — 3:55 AM: BeBo was never talking to the mother

Aly, on «مع بيبو، البيت أحلى»: **"Not البيت, it's a kids product."**

### The correction is one word wide and a section deep

**البيت is a mother's frame.** So is the whole BeBo voice section: *talking to the mother*, the feeling *permission without the wince*, a register described as **أم لأم**, and a words-in list of **ظرف · العيال · يكفّي · البيت**. Every one of those is a parent's vocabulary about children rather than a child's vocabulary about a drink.

### And I had been told the right answer three sessions ago

The client's discovery answer, in their own words: **"the kids buy them all mainly."**

**I read that, noticed it contradicted the voice guide, wrote the contradiction down, and then deferred to my own read of the packaging.** The line is still in the brief's history: *"it sits against BeBo talking to the mother... worth one more question rather than a rewrite."*

**That was the wrong call and it is the second time this week in the same shape.** The BeBo format question was the first: their statement against our inference, and I gave our inference the benefit of the doubt both times.

> **The rule, written into the brief so the next session inherits it: when the client states who buys it, that outranks what the packaging suggests.** A pack's characters tell you who the artwork is aimed at, not who is standing at the counter.

### What changed

BeBo now talks to **the kid**. The feeling is **«طعمي أنا»** — *it is mine and it tastes good* — rather than permission, **because a kid does not need permission, they need the thing to be worth asking for.** Register is kid to kid, 4 to 9 words, emoji free to three, person direct and never through a parent. **البيت and العيال moved from the words-in list to the words-out list.**

**Three of the four ranges now talk to the child, and AlRawy is the only adult-facing one** — which is exactly what §4 has said about AlRawy since it was written. The guide is more consistent after the correction than before it.

### The new BeBo line

**«مع بيبو، الفسحة أحلى»** — their **مع + أحلى** structure kept, but **الفسحة is the child's own word for the school break**, and school is the one occasion the client named themselves when they said it sells as an affordable juice for schools. **It names no format**, so it still survives whichever way the back-of-pack photo goes.
