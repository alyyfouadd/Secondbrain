# TSA — The Standard Agency

Everything for the agency: clients, offers, positioning, and the structure it scales on.

## What TSA is

We grow businesses on social media, and we find high-value businesses that need the right positioning in the market to grow faster — with AI doing the heavy lifting on execution.

**No packages.** Every client gets a dedicated price and a dedicated set of services, built around what that specific business needs. I can deliver all of them myself.

This is my fourth agency. The difference is that this one runs on direction, systems and structure instead of on me out-working the problem — and the system scales in parallel with the business rather than getting bolted on once it hurts.
