# Daily Brief

**One short brief a day, delivered without being asked.** Started 20 September 2026 at Aly's instruction.

> **What it is for: so the plan gets checked against reality every day instead of at the end of a week.** [[Alex Foods Delivery Plan]] has dates on it now, and **a plan nobody reads on a Tuesday is a plan that discovers on Friday that Tuesday did not happen.**

---

## The format — five lines, never more

**Short enough to read on a phone in ten seconds. A brief that takes a minute to read is a report, and reports get skipped.**

1. **The date, and days to delivery.** A live countdown, not a static one.
2. **Today's item from the plan.** One line, straight off [[Alex Foods Delivery Plan]].
3. **What moved since yesterday.** Read from the vault, not from memory.
4. **What is blocking, and who owes it.** **Named. "Waiting on the client" is not a blocker, it is an excuse** — say which person owes which thing.
5. **Money.** Anything due or overdue inside seven days.

**Then one line: the single most useful thing to do first today.** Not a list. One thing.

---

## The rules

- **Never invent progress.** Read the vault and say what is actually there. **If nothing moved, say nothing moved** — that is information, and a brief that always reports progress is a brief nobody believes.
- **Never pad it.** Five lines is a ceiling, not a target. **A quiet day gets a three-line brief.**
- **Never open with a greeting or close with an offer.** It is a status line, not a message.
- **Money gets named whenever it is inside seven days**, even if nothing changed, because **an invoice going quiet is how it goes unpaid.**
- **Overdue gets stated as overdue**, not as *still outstanding*.

---

## When it runs

**18:00 Cairo, daily.** Aly works nights and sleeps around 8 AM, so **six in the evening is the start of his working day, not the end of it.** A brief that lands at 9 AM arrives while he is asleep and is stale by the time he reads it.

**Scheduled as a Routine.** If the time turns out to be wrong, it is one edit rather than a rebuild.
