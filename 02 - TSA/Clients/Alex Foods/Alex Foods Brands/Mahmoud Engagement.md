# Mahmoud Engagement — the terms, the asks, the messages

**What TSA needs back from Mahmoud before he starts, and the messages that get it.** The design brief itself is [[Mahmoud Kit Brief]]; this note is the commercial conversation around it.

> **Two jobs, two money conversations, and they must not be merged.** The **kit** is one-off work against the Foundation's 20,000. The **graphics** are recurring work against Package A's 42,000/month. **Let him quote them as one bundle and neither number can ever be checked again** — and every graphics rate agreed casually for month 1 is a rate agreed against 126,000 EGP of term revenue ([[TSA Money]]).

---

## 1. What we actually need back — seven things

Ranked by what it costs to leave open.

| # | What | Why it is not a detail |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **A re-quote on the kit, against the real spec** | The 2,000 in [[Decisions]] #31 was priced for a supplementary kit. **He is now laying out ~46 pages of bilingual client-facing identity document.** A price agreed against the wrong job gets renegotiated at the worst moment |
| 2 | **Is 300 per ASSET or per DELIVERED POST?** | A feed post and its story crop are two files and one asset. **Twelve graphics a month is either 3,600 or 7,200**, repeated three times. **That single word is worth 10,800 across the term** *(calculated)* |
| 3 | **Will he design the six animated-still frames at his post rate?** | **The highest-leverage question on the whole delivery team.** At 1,800 it drops the animator from **eight originated pieces to two films plus six motion passes on finished art.** The animator is unfilled, is 8 of the 20 month-1 assets, and is the last blank in X. [[Month 1 Asset Brief]] §5 |
| 4 | **One Arabic page, before the whole document is committed** | **There is a rate for him and no evidence of his Arabic typesetting.** Arabic leading at 1.7 against Latin's 1.5 is the tell that separates a designer who reads Arabic from one who does not ([[Type System]]). One page costs a day now or fifteen days on 5 October |
| 5 | **Capacity against both workstreams** | The kit wants a first draft on **1 October** and month 1's twelve graphics land on top of it. **Month 1 starts when the school campaign goes live** ([[Decisions]] #26) and the client has never given that date — so the collision is real and unscheduled |
| 6 | **Who owns the working files** | Clause 5 reserves raw and project files as agency property **against the client.** It says nothing about a supplier. **Unagreed, the layered source of the client's identity document sits on a freelancer's laptop** |
| 7 | **His revision count, against the client's one** | The client gets one round under clause 2. **If Mahmoud also gets one, a single client note consumes it and the second costs money nobody budgeted** |

**And one thing that is told, not asked: no direct client relationship.** Clause 1 keeps execution TSA's and clause 5 keeps the method agency property — **the same rule already set for the media buyer** ([[Alex Foods]]).

## 2. Decide the walk-away number BEFORE asking

**Whatever he says first becomes the anchor.** Go in without a ceiling and the negotiation is already lost.

**The only honest reference point is his own rate**, because nothing in this vault holds Egyptian design market pricing and none should be invented.

- **His quoted rate is 300 per post.** At 46 pages that scale implies **several thousand, not 2,000** — a book page is not a post, but it is not an eighth of one either.
- **The stage is 20,000.** At 2,000 the Foundation runs a 90% margin; at 6,000 it is still 70% *(calculated)*.
- **So there is real room, and the risk is the opposite of the one being guarded against.** **An underpriced supplier on a three-month term is the one who walks in month 2**, when there is no bench and twenty assets are running.

> **The distinction that governs the whole conversation: he is told the SPEC and never the FEE.** Page count, bilingual, client-facing, the deadline, the revision structure — that is the brief. **What TSA charges Alex Foods is clause 5 material and a supplier never sees it.** Withholding the fee is normal; withholding the spec is how a 2,000 quote lands against a job he has not been shown.

## 3. The messages

**Send 3a, then 3b about an hour later.** Same day, deliberately: **he cannot answer the capacity question in 3b without having read the scope in 3a**, and drip-feeding money questions across a week to a supplier you need for three months reads as disorganised.

**Do not open with "the scope grew."** That invites a number before he has seen the work. **Let the brief make the case, then ask.**

### 3a · The kit

> يا محمود، معايا شغل ليك وعايز رأيك فيه قبل أي حاجة.
>
> **بنعمل دليل هوية كامل لأليكس فودز** — بنية العلامة، نظام الألوان، الخطوط، استخدام اللوجو والختم، وصفحتين لكل مجموعة من الأربعة، وكمان المقاسات وقواعد وضع العبوة على الخلفية والشارات.
>
> **حوالي ٤٦ صفحة، A4، عربي وإنجليزي والعربي هو الأساس في التصميم.** المستند ده بيروح للعميل نفسه، فمش شغل داخلي.
>
> **كل الكلام والألوان والملفات جاهزة عندي ومكتوبة بالتفصيل** — مش هتكتب ولا تختار لون ولا خط. إنت بتصمم الصفحات بس. مرفق البريف كامل.
>
> اقراه وقوللي حاجتين:
>
> **١. السعر** على الشغل ده بالظبط.
> **٢. المعاد** — محتاج أول نسخة **١ أكتوبر**، وجولة تعديل واحدة بعدها.
>
> وقبل ما نمشي، عايز **صفحة واحدة تجريبية بالعربي** — أي صفحة من المستند — عشان نتفق على الشكل من الأول بدل ما نكتشف حاجة في النص.

**English gloss:** I've got work for you and I want your read before anything else. We're building a full identity guide for Alex Foods — architecture, colour, type, logo and seal use, two pages per range across four ranges, plus sizes, the pack-on-field rules and the badges. About 46 pages, A4, Arabic and English with Arabic leading the design. It goes to the client, so it isn't internal work. All copy, colours and files are ready and written out in detail — you're not writing, choosing a colour or picking a typeface. You design the pages. Full brief attached. Read it and tell me two things: the price for exactly this, and the timing — I need a first draft 1 October with one revision round after. And before we start, I want one test page in Arabic, any page, so we agree the look up front instead of discovering something halfway.

### 3b · The month, and the question worth the most

> وحاجة تانية منفصلة، عن شغل الشهر:
>
> **١.** الـ٣٠٠ اللي اتفقنا عليها — **دي للأصل الواحد ولا للبوست المسلّم؟** يعني لو البوست له نسخة ستوري، دول واحد ولا اتنين؟ عايز أفهمها صح من الأول عشان محاسبتنا تمشي مظبوطة تلات شهور.
>
> **٢.** وده الأهم: عندنا **٦ صور متحركة** في الشهر. الحركة نفسها هيعملها حد تاني، **بس الفريم المصمم نفسه ممكن تعمله إنت بسعر البوست العادي؟** لو أيوة، ده بيوفر علينا كتير وبيخليك إنت اللي ماسك شكل الشهر كله.
>
> **٣.** وآخر حاجة: الاتنين دول — الدليل والاتناشر تصميم — **هينزلوا قريبين من بعض.** تقدر تاخد الاتنين ولا نظبط المواعيد؟ قوللي بصراحة أحسن دلوقتي.

**English gloss:** A separate thing, about the monthly work. One: the 300 we agreed — is that per asset or per delivered post? If a post has a story version, is that one or two? I want to get it right from the start so the accounting runs clean for three months. Two, and this is the important one: we have six animated stills a month. Someone else does the motion, but could you do the designed frame itself at your normal post rate? If yes it saves us a lot and it puts you in control of how the whole month looks. Three: these two jobs — the guide and the twelve graphics — land close together. Can you take both, or do we stagger them? Tell me straight now rather than later.

**Why 3b is structured this way:**

- **The 300 question is first because it is the boring one**, and a supplier who answers something administrative is now in the conversation.
- **The still frames are framed as control, not as a saving.** *"It puts you in control of how the whole month looks"* is true and it is what a designer actually wants. **"It saves us money" is true too and is an argument for TSA, not for him.**
- **The capacity question invites a no.** *"Tell me straight now rather than later"* is the only version that gets an honest answer — **and an honest no in September is worth more than a yes that fails on 5 October.**

---

## 4. What is NOT in these messages, and why

- **The Foundation's 20,000 and Package A's 42,000.** Clause 5. §2 above.
- **That the old 2,000 is being revisited.** Naming it anchors him to it or tells him TSA thinks it was wrong — **the brief and a fresh ask do the job without either.**
- **The three open identity decisions** — the seal, the master blue, POLEKA's extended colours ([[Mahmoud Kit Brief]] §7). **They block §06 and the POLEKA spread only**, and everything else can start. **Handing a supplier a list of things his client has not decided invites him to wait.** They go to him as answers when they exist.
- **Working files, revisions and the no-client-contact rule.** Real and non-negotiable — **but they are terms for the conversation after he quotes**, not conditions stapled to the first ask. Attaching them to the opener reads as distrust of somebody who has not done anything yet.
