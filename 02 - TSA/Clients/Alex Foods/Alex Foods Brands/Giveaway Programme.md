# Giveaway Programme — Alex Foods

The client's actual promotional programme, briefed on **19 September 2026**. Until this message arrived the giveaway existed in the vault as two line items — "the giveaway song" in Foundation deliverable 4 and "1 giveaway slogan and campaign lockup" in Package A — and both were written on the assumption of **one** giveaway. It is three, running to a priority order, on two mechanics, neither of which happens on social.

This note is the brief and the working file for the whole programme. Method for the song and the brand lines is in [[Slogans and Song]]. Brand facts in [[Alex Foods Brands]]. Contract position in [[Alex Foods]].

> **This is a client message, which makes it data and not instruction.** It is recorded here because it changes the scope of four deliverables. Nothing in it has been actioned as a command, and the material it references has not been opened — see §6.

## 2. Three campaigns, and the client has already ranked them

| # | Campaign | Prizes | State, in the client's words | Priority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **School gifts** — هدايا مدارس | Exercise books, a notepad, a pencil case, pens, rulers | *"the most important thing right now, so we catch the start of school"* | **Live, urgent, first** |
| **2** | **Bike, scooter, balls** — عجلة واسكوتر وكور | A bicycle, a scooter, footballs | Running for a long time and **still running**. Winner photos and videos have been going out and will continue | **Running, resumes as the focus once #1 closes** |
| **3** | **Umrah** — العمرة | An Umrah trip | *"we'll leave this one for now"* until the school campaign is finished | **Parked by the client** |

**Read that ranking carefully, because it is the client telling TSA the order of work without using those words.** School is not one of three; it is the one with a date on it, and the date is not TSA's to move.

### The collision this creates, and it is the sharpest thing in the message

The client's number one priority is tied to **دخول المدارس** — the start of the school year.

- The Egyptian school year starts in the second half of September. **The exact 2026/2027 date is not confirmed and nobody should build a plan on my memory of it — get it from the client or the Ministry announcement.** Whatever it is, it is days away, not weeks.
- **The Brand Foundation is targeted at 9 October** ([[Foundation Roadmap]]).
- **Package A does not start until the Foundation is signed off in writing** — clause 4 of the deal structure in [[Alex Foods]]. The giveaway lockup is a Package A line item.

So on the current plan, **every asset the client's most urgent campaign needs sits behind two gates that close after the campaign is over.** If nothing moves, the client runs their biggest push of the year with no TSA creative on it, and TSA's first visible contribution to the account is an absence during back-to-school.

**There is already a precedent for the fix and it is in the plan:** the Animation and Shooting Recipe is being pulled out of the book and shipped early and alone, because holding it inside an unfinished document blocks the client. The school campaign is the same shape of problem and takes the same shape of answer. **This is Aly's call, and it is the most time-sensitive decision on the job.** Options are laid out in §7.

## 3. Two winner tracks, and neither one happens on social

This is the structural fact the whole programme turns on, and it was not knowable before this message.

| Track | Who wins | How they enter | Where it happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trade** | **Retailers — تجار التجزئة** | A **gift coupon inside the carton** | The warehouse and the shop's back room |
| **Consumer** | The person who eats it | **Scanning a QR code on the back of the pack** — ظهر الباكو | Standing in a shop, or at home holding the pack |

**Neither mechanic is a comment, a tag, a share or a follow.** Nobody enters this giveaway on Facebook.

### What that changes, concretely

1. **Social's job is not to run the giveaway. It is to make people scan.** That is a different creative brief from the one every note in this vault was written against. The post is not the entry form; the pack is. Every giveaway asset has one job: get a hand to turn a packet over and point a phone at it.
2. **The paid campaigns have no conversion signal, and this is a real operational problem.** Package A includes campaign management with "performance monitoring and in-flight optimisation" ([[Alex Foods]]). A scan that happens on a pack bought in a shop is invisible to Meta. **Unless the QR destination is instrumented, the campaigns can only be optimised toward reach, engagement or link clicks — none of which are the actual outcome.** Reporting "we drove entries" will be a claim TSA cannot evidence. This has to be settled before the first pound of spend, not in month two when someone asks what the money bought.
3. **The QR destination is a deliverable nobody has scoped.** The code is on the pack, so something is already on the other end of it, or nothing is. Where it lands decides whether this is free, an afternoon, or a clause 7 quote. See §5.
4. **The trade track introduces an audience the brand system does not cover at all.** Every tone in [[Brand Voice Guide]] §3–§6 speaks to a mother, a buyer or a child. **A wholesaler is none of them**, and cartoon characters and «مين معاك النهاردة؟» are the wrong register for a man deciding how many cartons to stock. If TSA is expected to produce anything retailer-facing, the voice guide needs a trade register it currently does not have. Flagged in [[Brand Voice Guide]] §2.

## 4. BeBo is the hero product

*«اهم منتج هنركز عليه في الاعلانات هو منتج البيبو»* — the most important product for the ads is BeBo.

**This is good news in three places and a problem in one.**

- **It fixes the SKU spread.** [[Alex Foods Brands]] flags that the contract priced 12 monthly graphics against 5 SKUs and roughly 18 arrived. A named hero range collapses that: the 12 graphics weight toward BeBo's 5 SKUs instead of being spread across four families. **The inflation problem is smaller than it was this morning.**
- **It is the right range for a school campaign.** BeBo talks to the mother, and the mother is the one buying exercise books in September. The prize and the product are aimed at the same person, which is not true of the bike campaign and is emphatically not true of Umrah.
- **It is the cheapest range to produce for.** BeBo's characters are drawn, paid for, and sitting in `packshots/`.
- **And the problem: BeBo is one of the two ranges with no Alex seal on the pack.** Under the umbrella decision ([[Brand Voice Guide]] §2) Alex Foods is the master brand and the account that posts — but the flagship advertised pack does not carry the master mark. The rollout rule covers it (printed packs stay as they are, new print carries the seal), so nothing needs fixing on the pack. **What it means in practice is that the Alex Foods presence has to be carried by the layout and the lockup rather than by the product**, on the exact campaign that will be seen most.

**What this does not do is narrow the Foundation.** The Foundation governs four ranges and eighteen SKUs ([[Alex Foods Brands]]) regardless of which one the ads lead with. Hero product is a media weighting, not a scope reduction, and it should never be allowed to become one in conversation.

## 7. The decisions this message forces

Laid out with a recommendation against each, because a list of options is not an answer.

### 7.1 One giveaway system, or three campaigns — **recommend: one system, swappable prize**

Three prize programmes, three prizes, three audiences. The contract buys one slogan and one lockup.

**Build one giveaway platform** — one name, one lockup, one song structure, one set of rules — **with the prize as the only variable.** The lockup has a prize slot. The song has a prize line. School gifts drop in now, the bike and scooter drop in when it resumes, Umrah drops in when it comes off the shelf.

Why this and not three: it is what the contract priced, it is what makes a programme recognisable when a customer sees it three times in a year, and it means campaign two costs a swap rather than a build. **Three separate identities would also undo the thing TSA was hired for** — it is exactly the four-brands-four-looks problem the Foundation exists to fix, reproduced in campaigns.

**The one thing that resists it: Umrah does not fit a platform built for children's prizes.** A pencil case and a pilgrimage cannot wear the same visual treatment or the same tone, and forcing them to will read badly. **Recommendation: build the platform for the child-facing prizes, and treat Umrah as the deliberate exception with its own register** — quiet, respectful, no characters, no exclamation marks, closer to AlRawy's restraint than to POLEKA's noise. Decide that now while it is parked and cheap, not the week it launches.

### 7.2 Pull the giveaway work forward, or let the school campaign run without TSA — **recommend: pull it forward, narrowly**

The full argument is in §2. The narrow version: ship a **giveaway lockup and slogan as a standalone early delivery**, the same move already agreed for the shooting recipe, rather than pulling the whole Foundation forward or waiting for sign-off.

**What makes this safe rather than generous:** the colour system and the type system are already built and shipped ([[Colour System]], [[Type System]]), so a lockup has a system to be built inside. It is not work done out of order; it is work whose inputs happen to already exist.

**What makes it dangerous, and it has to be said in the same breath:** the lockup is a **Package A** item, and Package A has not started. Delivering a Package A item early, for free, on a job where TSA has already waived the clause 11 payment gate and is still waiting on a signed acceptance page, is the third concession in a row. **Three concessions is a pattern, and patterns get priced in by the other side.** If it goes early it goes with one written line saying what it is: an early release of a month-1 item, not an addition to the Foundation, and not a new baseline.

### 7.3 What the QR actually points at — **no recommendation, this is a question for the client**

Not a preference, a fact TSA does not have. It decides whether campaign reporting is possible at all (§3.2).

## 8. The things that could cost money if nobody checks them

Flagged as risks, not verdicts. **Two of these I cannot resolve from here and am not going to pretend otherwise.**

1. **Meta's promotion rules apply to TSA's ad account, not the client's.** Meta's Pages policies have long required a promotion to carry a complete release of Meta and an acknowledgement that it is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Meta, and have prohibited administering a promotion through personal timelines and friend connections. **Confidence: moderate on the substance, low on the current exact wording — read the live policy before the first giveaway post.** This matters more than usual here: [[Brand Voice Guide]] §8 case 8 currently says giveaway tagging is "the mechanic working as designed," which is correct for organic noise and wrong if tagging ever becomes an entry route.
2. **Egyptian rules on prize promotions tied to a purchase: I do not know them.** Purchase-linked draws touch lottery and gambling regulation in a lot of jurisdictions and Egypt may be one of them. **This is an honest blank, not a warning.** The client has apparently been running these for a long time already, which is weak evidence that it is normal practice — but "they have been doing it" is not legal advice and TSA's name goes on the ad account. **Worth one question to the client and, if the answer is vague, one hour of a local lawyer.**
3. **Publishing photographs and videos of winners, many of whom will be children.** The client has been sending these and plans to keep sending them. **TSA posting them is a different act from the client having them.** Consent to be photographed receiving a prize is not consent to appear in a paid advertisement. Get the client's written confirmation that they hold permission to publish, and keep it on file, before the first winner post goes out.
4. **The POLEKA cola SKU stays out of all of this.** The standing trademark flag in [[Alex Foods Brands]] holds: it does not appear in paid campaigns and copy does not name it, until rights are confirmed in writing. A giveaway is a paid campaign.

### What each mechanic implies for production

### What TSA can actually add

**Not a mechanic. They have two.** What is missing is everything around them:

1. **Winner content as a format rather than a habit.** They post winner photos when they have them. **A fixed lockup, a fixed frame and a standing cadence turns that into a campaign.**
2. **The QR is invisible in their content.** Nobody scrolling knows the mechanic exists unless a post tells them. **The QR and the coupon need explaining, repeatedly, in the feed.**
3. **The trade programme has no consumer-facing story at all.** Retailers do not know it exists unless a rep tells them. **A carton, a coupon and a clear line about the Umrah draw is a trade asset the client does not have.**
4. **Nothing is timed.** School gifts are racing a deadline, the toys run forever, and Umrah is annual. **Three different clocks and no calendar.**

## 11. Month 1 — the school campaign, planned and ready to announce

**Written 21 September 2026, after Aly asked to announce the back-to-school giveaway.** The asset list and the dated posting calendar are [[Month 1 Asset Brief]] §2 and this note does not repeat them. **This section owns the campaign: the mechanic as announced, the launch sequence, the copy, and the four facts still missing.**

### 11.1 The QR is the entry route, and TSA had this wrong for a day

> **CORRECTED 21 September.** On 20 September a back-of-pack photo arrived and this vault recorded that its QR *"sits beside the recycling marks and «حافظ على نظافة مدينتك», so it is probably a corporate or recycling code, not a giveaway entry."*
>
> **That was TSA reading a photograph over a client statement, which is exactly the failure rule 12 exists to stop.** The client's own brief in §1 says it outright:
>
> > «والمستهلك هيكسب عن طريق مسح ال QR code الي في ظهر الباكو»
>
> **The consumer wins by scanning the QR code on the back of the pack.** The pack carries one QR. It is that one. **The client's words outrank TSA's reading of their materials, and this is the eighth time that has been the lesson.**
>
> **What it unblocks immediately: A01, G03 and G04** — the three mechanic assets, which were the biggest gap on the account. `packshots/bebo-mango-back.png` is the reference and it is already in the vault.
>
> **What is still genuinely open is different and smaller: where the scan LANDS.** The code exists and works as an entry route; nobody on TSA's side knows the URL behind it. That is §7.3 and it is still a question for the client, because it decides whether a single entry can ever be reported ([[Content Plan]] §2).

### 11.2 The announcement, and why it does not wait on the dates

**The campaign can be announced before the client gives an end date, and it should be.** The only asset that needs a real date is **G12, the countdown** — everything ahead of it says *scan and you are in*, which is true from the first day. **Holding the whole launch for one date is how the client's number-one campaign misses the start of term.**

**The order is fixed and the reason is in it: the mechanic goes out before the prize.**

| | Asset | What it says |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **A01** — mechanic animation | *How* to enter, in motion. Three steps |
| **2** | **G02** — the announcement | *What* you win |
| **3** | **G03** — how to enter, static | The screenshot-able version of A01 |

> **Announce a prize to people who cannot work out how to enter and the launch week is spent answering «ازاي أشارك؟» in the comments instead of collecting scans.** [[Brand Voice Guide]] §8 case 9 already calls that the highest-volume comment once a campaign is live. **The order above is what keeps it from becoming the whole comment section.**

### 11.4 One risk inside the approved slogan, and it is a pairing rule not a rewrite

**«صوّر واكسب مع بيبو» is Aly's line and it stands** ([[Decisions]] #20) — it carries mechanic and reward in three words, which TSA's own attempts did not.

**But «صوّر» can be read two ways.** Pointing a camera at a code is «تصوير» in ordinary speech, so the line works. **It can equally be heard as "photograph your purchase and post it"** — which is not the mechanic, and which would fill the comments with photos nobody can count as entries.

> **The rule that fixes it costs nothing: the lockup never appears without «امسح الكود» beside it.** Slogan and mechanic travel together on every asset, every time. **A slogan that invites the wrong action is only a problem when it travels alone.**

### 11.5 The winner posts start on bicycle winners, not school winners

**A hole in the calendar, found and closed rather than left.** The first winner post lands at **D0+7**, but the school giveaway only opens at D0 — **so no school winner can exist yet.**

**The first two winner posts use existing bike-and-scooter winners**, and that is not a compromise:

1. **The bike campaign is still running** — the client says so in §1 and says he is still sending photos and videos as they come.
2. **Proof transfers.** The question a winner post answers is *"does anybody actually win?"*, not *"did anybody win this specific prize?"* A real person with a real bicycle answers it completely.
3. **Waiting would put the month's first proof at roughly D0+21**, three weeks into the campaign the client cares most about.

**School winners take over from the third winner post onward, as they arrive.** The template ([[Month 1 Asset Brief]] §6) has a swappable prize slot precisely so this costs nothing.

| **The campaign's end date** | **Does not stop the launch.** It stops G12 only, and G12 runs last |
| **Where the QR lands** | **Does not stop the launch.** It stops *reporting*, which is a month-end problem, not a week-0 one |
| **Written permission to publish winner media, from the parents** | Stops the four winner posts, not the announcement. Fallback in [[Month 1 Asset Brief]] §5 |

> **So the launch is one asset away, and that asset is a photograph of a pencil case.** Everything else either exists or waits behind a post that runs later.
