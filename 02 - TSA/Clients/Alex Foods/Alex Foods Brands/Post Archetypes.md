# Post Archetypes — Alex Foods

> **Book section 18, the pattern inventory.** Split from [[Content Plan]] on 20 September, when two sessions wrote a content plan each. **That note holds the strategy — the term, the twenty assets, the engagement goal. This one holds the patterns:** why a post exists at all, and the seven archetypes with what is fixed, what changes and what is never done. **It is the section that makes the book buildable rather than admirable.**

**What to post, why, and what it looks like.** Book sections 17 and 18. Written 20 September 2026, **once enough was known about the business to write it honestly.**

> **The spine is the campaigns, not the ranges.** TSA had been planning a four-range rotation. **The client runs three campaigns on three different clocks** — school gifts racing a deadline, toys running continuously, the Umrah trade programme paused. **Ranges rotate inside campaigns, never the other way round.**

---

## 1. Why post at all — the three jobs

**Every post does one of these. A post that does none of them does not go out.**

| Job | Serves | How much |
|---|---|---|
| **Prove the giveaway is real** | **The contracted KPI: comments and views.** Winner content is the highest-trust thing this brand owns. | **~35%** |
| **Explain how to enter** | **Conversion.** The mechanic is printed on the pack and invisible in the feed. | **~20%** |
| **Keep the products moving** | 18 SKUs, the flavour rotation, the characters | **~35%** |
| **Serve the trade** | **«Mostly more shops»**, which is half the stated goal and currently has no content at all | **~10%** |

**This replaces the 40/30/20/10 mix in the Brand and Social Kit.** That mix was derived from packaging before anyone knew there was a live giveaway programme or a trade loyalty scheme. **It was a reasonable guess and it is now out of date.**

## 2. The seven post archetypes

**Each one: what it is, why it exists, what is fixed, what changes.**

### A · The Winner Post
> **The best content this brand owns, and they already make it.**

**Why.** A real person holding a real bicycle outside a real shop is proof, and **proof outperforms every claim a food brand is allowed to make.** It is also the only content here that answers *"is this giveaway actually real?"*, which is the question stopping people entering.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | A real winner, a real prize, shot in the shop. **«مبروك للفايز» lockup, same position every time.** Alex seal. First name of the winner. |
| **Changes** | The person, the prize, the shop, the caption |
| **Never** | A stock photo. A staged winner. **A prize the person is not holding.** |

**Caption shape:** congratulate by name · what they bought · the invitation. *«الف مبروك لإياد ❤️ اشرب بيبو.. دورك جاي»* — **their own structure, and it works.**

> **Built as a reusable template, 21 September, and it lives in [[Month 1 Asset Brief]] §6** — the anatomy, the source-photo accept-and-reject gate, the Umrah variant, the motion version and the consent rules. **Four a month from one locked master**, because this post repeats every week across all three campaigns. **This archetype says what the post is; that section says how it gets built.**

### B · The Mechanic Explainer
> **The biggest gap on the account. Nobody scrolling knows how to enter.**

**Why.** The QR code is on the back of the pack and **every post they publish shows the front.** A giveaway nobody knows how to enter is a giveaway that converts on the shelf only.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | **The back of the pack, QR legible at phone size.** Three steps maximum. One instruction per frame. |
| **Changes** | Range, prize shown, format (still or animation) |
| **Never** | A QR too small to scan. More than three steps. **The front of the pack as the hero.** |

**This is the one to spend an animation on.** Two a month are funded, and **an animated three-step explainer is worth more than a second product film.**

### C · The Product Post
**Why.** Eighteen SKUs, and the pack is the creative. Keeps the flavour rotation visible and the range front of mind.

**Fixed:** pack on its own flavour field · 8% margin · type at the foot · **computed type colour, never chosen.**
**Changes:** the SKU, the field, the caption.

### D · The Character Post
**Why.** **The cheapest content this business owns — already drawn, already paid for.** BeBo's faces, POLEKA's animals, 2MAN's boy. A competitor cannot copy them.

**Fixed:** character leads the frame · **a POLEKA post without a character is off-brand even if the copy is perfect.**
**Changes:** which character, the line.

### E · The Moment Post
**Why.** The occasion, never a claim about it. **The school bag, the hot afternoon, the shop fridge.** It is what makes a product feel like part of a day rather than an object.

**Fixed:** a real context · **no health cue, no fruit pile, no nutritional implication.**
**Changes:** the moment, the range.

### F · The Trade Post
> **Nothing on this account currently talks to a shop owner. Half the stated goal is «more shops».**

**Why.** Retailers win through a coupon in the carton and through the Umrah programme, **and neither has ever been explained publicly.** A distributor deciding whether to carry a carton has nothing to look at.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Fixed** | **The carton, not the single pack.** The coupon. Margin language, case counts, the Alexandria heritage. |
| **Changes** | The programme, the range, the ask |
| **Never** | A child. **This post is not talking to them.** |

**Blocked until the carton and coupon artwork arrive.**

### G · The Countdown
**Why.** Only during a campaign with a deadline. **School gifts have one; the toys do not.** Urgency is a campaign lever and using it outside a campaign spends it.

**Fixed:** a real date or a real limit · **«الكمية محدودة» only when it is true.**
**Changes:** the campaign.

## 3. Month 1 — the school campaign

**Twenty assets, which is what Package A funds.** 2 animations, 6 animated stills, 12 graphics.

| Archetype | Count | Format |
|---|---|---|
| **Winner post** | **4** | 1 animated still + 3 graphics · **one a week, and it anchors the month** |
| **Mechanic explainer** | **3** | **1 animation** + 2 graphics |
| **Product post** | **5** | 2 animated stills + 3 graphics |
| **Character post** | **3** | 2 animated stills + 1 graphic |
| **Moment — school** | **3** | **1 animation** + 1 animated still + 1 graphic |
| **Trade post** | **2** | 2 graphics *(blocked on carton artwork)* |
| **Total** | **20** | 2 animations · 6 animated stills · 12 graphics |

**Where the two animations go, and why:** one on the **mechanic explainer**, because it is the biggest gap and motion explains a three-step process better than a static frame ever will. One on the **school moment**, because it is the campaign the client is racing and it deserves the month's best asset.

### The rhythm

**Facebook 4 to 5 a week, TikTok 2, Instagram mirrors.** Derived from 20 assets across 4.35 weeks, **not from ambition.**

**Weekly shape:** the winner post anchors the week · one mechanic or product post mid-week · one character or moment post · the rest rotates.

---

## 4. What changes when the campaign changes

| Campaign | Archetype weighting |
|---|---|
| **School gifts** *(now)* | **Moment and Countdown rise.** The deadline is real and the occasion is specific. |
| **Bicycles and scooters** *(ongoing)* | **Winner and Mechanic dominate.** No deadline, so urgency is off and proof carries it. |
| **Umrah** *(paused)* | **Trade becomes the lead**, and the consumer archetypes drop to maintenance. |

> **This is the part nobody had: three campaigns, three clocks, and now a weighting for each.**

---

## 5. What is still blocking this plan

| Blocker | Blocks |
|---|---|
| **Carton and coupon artwork** | **Both trade posts.** Nothing can be made for a shop owner without them. |
| **Back-of-pack shot with a legible QR** | **All three mechanic explainers**, which is the biggest gap on the account |
| **Seasonality** | **Months 2 and 3.** Half the portfolio is frozen and the term runs into winter. |
| **Meta access** | Publishing, and the baseline the whole thing is measured against |
