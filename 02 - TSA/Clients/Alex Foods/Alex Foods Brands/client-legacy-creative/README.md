# client-legacy-creative — the "before", and the client's own voice

**What Alex Foods looks like and sounds like in market today**, kept as the reference the new system argues against, and as the only primary source on how the client actually writes.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `legacy-taam-ahla.jpg` | Their own market creative leading with «طعم أحلى مع ALEX FOODS». **This is the evidence behind the umbrella decision** — the parent running as a consumer brand with every range's products arranged underneath. |
| `legacy-mini-sizes.jpg` | The mini 2MAN and mini BeBo carton creative. **Two SKUs nobody had counted**, and the source of the **25 YEARS** badge that contradicts the seal's «منذ 20 عامًا». |
| `client-social-copy.md` | **Two of their own published posts, recorded verbatim.** The first primary source on the client's voice, and it outranks every voice inference in the vault, because the guide was written off packaging and this is the client speaking. |

## Why these are kept rather than discarded

**They are the "before" the do-not pages need.** A governance document that only shows the right answer teaches less than one that shows what it replaced. And `client-social-copy.md` did real work: it settled BeBo as a juice, corrected 2MAN's social register to nostalgia aimed at adults, and validated the انتعاش instruction as an echo of the client's own words rather than a preference.

**Raster, and theirs.** Never re-rendered, never edited, never used as artwork.
