# Local SEO — Alexandria

**Foundation deliverable 8 for [[Alex Foods]].** The signed scope's words: *"business data and keywords tuned to rank inside Alexandria and surrounding areas."* This note is the content of that deliverable and it becomes book §20, which ships in TSA's Operating Guide ([[Decisions]] #37). Where it has got to is in [[Delivery Register]], not here.

**Three parts, which is what the scope buys:** what is already out there about the company (§2), the one set of business data every surface uses from now on (§3), and the keywords, split by who is searching (§4). §5 is the competitor read and §6 is the monthly routine Package A pays for.

---

## 1. What local SEO can and cannot do for a factory

**Nobody searches for a 75 ml drink pouch "near me".** A child spending pocket money buys whatever sits in the corner shop's rack or freezer, and no search is involved. **So this deliverable does not move consumer sales, and it is not presented as if it might.** On top of that, distribution is national and this deliverable only covers Alexandria ([[Alex Foods Discovery Brief]] §A5).

**Four groups do search, and the deliverable is built for them:**

| Who | What they type | What they need to find | Why it matters to the client |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A shop owner or trader looking for a supplier** | «مصنع عصير اسكندرية», «عصير اكياس جملة» | A real factory, a phone number that answers, and directions | **This is the "more shops" goal** they gave us, and the only commercial job this deliverable can actually do |
| **Anyone who has seen the brand** | «عصير بيبو», «تومان», «Alex Foods» | The official page and the right contact details, not a stranger's post or a dead website | Whoever owns the brand-name results owns the first impression |
| **Drivers, distributors' staff, suppliers, job seekers** | The factory name, «أبيس», the road | A correct pin on the map | A factory on an agricultural road with a wrong pin costs a lost truck a day |
| **A parent checking the product** | The brand name plus «سعر» or «مكونات» | Official information | The answers come under [[Brand Voice Guide]]'s never-say list. **This is where an unsupported claim hurts most** |

**The line the delivery message carries, word for word in spirit:** *this makes Alex Foods findable and correct on Google in Alexandria, above all for traders. It will not sell pouches nationally, and we will not report it as if it did.*

| | «طريق إسكندرية مصر الزراعي، مدخل خورشيد، أبيس الأولى» | What the client told us ([[Alex Foods Discovery Brief]] §2) |
| | "…khorshid entrance to the first apis" | F3 |
| | «الفرزدق» | F4, unverified |
| **Phone** | 01010000015 · 01010001374 · WhatsApp **015**50210766 | Printed on the pack |

## 3. The business data master — one version, everywhere

**This is the deliverable's core.** Once it is settled, every surface copies it character for character: the Google Business Profile, the Facebook About section, Instagram, TikTok, any directory TSA corrects, and any new website. **Every field below is either decided or it is a question for the client. None of it is guessed.**

| Field | Master value | State |
|---|---|---|
| **Business name on Google** | **Alex Foods** | **Recommended.** Google requires the name the business actually uses publicly, and bans keywords stuffed into it. Alex Foods is the master brand ([[Decisions]]), the name on the social accounts and the name traders know. **Never** «Alex Foods – عصير أطفال جملة الإسكندرية»: that is exactly the keyword-stuffing Google suspends profiles for. **Confirm it matches the sign on the factory gate** |
| **Legal name** | ? | **Question:** «لتصنيع» or «لتعبئة»? Take it from the commercial register, not from us. It goes in the description, never in the name field |
| **Address, Arabic** | طريق إسكندرية القاهرة الزراعي، مدخل خورشيد، أبيس الأولى، الإسكندرية | Built from the client's own wording. **Confirm the district** (the pack says «رمل ثاني») |
| **Address, English** | Cairo–Alexandria Agricultural Road, Khorshid Entrance, Abis 1, Alexandria | Same source |
| **Map pin** | Dropped on the **gate a truck actually uses**, not the middle of the plot | **Needs someone who knows the site.** Mohamed drops it from his phone, or sends a photo of the gate |
| **Primary phone** | ? | **Question:** one number that a person answers during business hours. **Recommendation: the customer-service line already printed on millions of packs**, because it is already public |
| **WhatsApp** | ? | **Question:** 0155… or 0105…? See §2 |
| **Website** | **Blank, or the Facebook page** | **Never a dead domain.** A profile linking to a site that does not load is worse than one with no link. **Question:** do they still own `alex-foods.com`? |
| **Hours** | ? | **Question, and a bigger one than it looks:** what hours can a trader actually visit or call? **Google only lists businesses that deal with people in person during the hours they state** — a factory qualifies if it receives traders and suppliers. **If nobody receives visitors, the profile has to be set up as a service-area business with the address hidden**, and the map pin goes with it |
| **Primary category** | **Food manufacturer** *(or the closest match on Google's live list)* | **Recommended.** Confirm against Google's live category list when the profile is opened; the list can only be seen from inside the profile |
| **Secondary categories** | Beverage distributor · Food products supplier · Ice cream manufacturer *(for 2MAN and POLEKA, if it is on the list)* | Recommended, same caveat. **Never "Juice shop"**: see §4, cluster D |
| **Description, Arabic first** | Drafted below | Written, and it passes the never-say list |

### The description

> **أليكس فودز، من الإسكندرية لكل مصر من أكتر من ٢٠ سنة.** بنصنع بيبو، والراوي، وتومان، وبوليكا: مشروبات ونكتار وآيس بوب وعصير مجمد. للتجار والموزعين: كلمونا على الرقم أو واتساب.
>
> *Alex Foods, from Alexandria, for all of Egypt, for more than 20 years. Makers of BeBo, AlRawy, 2MAN and POLEKA: drinks, nectar, ice pops and frozen juice. Traders and distributors: call or WhatsApp.*

**Why every word is there, and why some are not:**
- **«أكتر من ٢٠ سنة», never 25.** [[Alex Foods Discovery Brief]] §2.
- **«مشروبات» for BeBo, not «عصير»**, until [[Decisions]] #30 is settled. **Keywords push hard towards «عصير»** (§4, cluster A), which is why that open question matters here too.
- **«نكتار» for AlRawy, never «عصير طبيعي» and never «أورجانيك»**: [[Brand Voice Guide]]'s never-say list.
- **The trader line is the whole commercial point.** It aims the profile at the one searcher who can open a shop.
- **The legal name gets added once §3 settles which version is correct.**

## 4. The keyword map — Alexandria

**No search volumes, and this is not an oversight.** Every free keyword tool (Google Keyword Planner, Google Trends, Google's own autocomplete) is blocked from here. **So the clusters below come from how the search results looked on 23 September, and the ranking comes from reasoning, not from numbers.** The free way to get real volumes is Google Keyword Planner, which needs nothing but a Google Ads account with no spend. **It becomes possible as soon as the Google access arrives, and it is the first thing to run once it does.**

### A · Brand names — own these first

**The cheapest ground there is: nobody else is trying to rank for these words.**

| Search | What comes up now | The job |
|---|---|---|
| عصير بيبو · بيبو · bebo juice | **Their own Facebook posts and TikTok search pages** (F2, F6) | Put the Google profile and the verified page at the top. **The client already uses «عصير» here, which is exactly what #30 has to rule on** |
| تومان · 2man ايس · تومان ايس بوب | Not checked | Check from the iPad |
| بوليكا · بوليكا جيلي | **Nothing of theirs** (F7) | **Invisible.** The profile and page must name POLEKA in their text, in Arabic |
| الراوي نكتار · الراوي عصير | **Other brands and nectar explainers** (F7) | Invisible, and «الراوي» is a common word, so it will always be crowded. **Always pair it: «الراوي نكتار»** |
| أليكس فودز · Alex Foods · the legal name | A dead website, a directory, a second Facebook page (F1–F4) | **Fix the wrong versions before adding new ones** |

### B · Trade and suppliers in Alexandria — the "more shops" cluster

**The only cluster that earns money for the client. These are the people who can open new shops.**

| Search | Intent |
|---|---|
| مصنع عصير اسكندرية · مصنع عصائر في الاسكندرية | Looking for a factory to buy from |
| عصير اكياس جملة · عصير اطفال جملة · عصير اكياس كرتونة | Buying by the carton, for a shop |
| ايس بوب جملة · مصنع ايس بوب · آيس بوب اسكندرية | Freezer stock, buying for a shop |
| موزع عصائر اسكندرية · توكيل عصير | Wants to become a distributor, **the most valuable single enquiry this deliverable can produce** |

**What wins these:** the right categories (§3), the description's trader line, **Google profile posts written to traders once a month** (§6), and a phone number that someone actually answers. **The same words go in the Facebook About section.**

> **Who ranks now:** «مصنع عصير اسكندرية» returns a fresh-juice supplier to hotels (Al Prince), directory category pages, and a news story about a raided unlicensed juice factory. **None of them is a packaged-kids-drink maker.** **That gap is winnable, because nobody in this category is trying for it.**

### C · Location and directions

| Search | Who |
|---|---|
| The factory name + «أبيس» / «طريق مصر اسكندرية الزراعي» / «مدخل خورشيد» | Drivers, suppliers, job seekers, distributors' staff |

**One correct pin wins all of these.** No content work.

### D · Excluded on purpose, with the reason

| Search | Why it is not targeted |
|---|---|
| **عصير اسكندرية · محل عصير · عصائر طازجة** | **These are searches for fresh-juice shops.** The results are directory lists of juice bars in Miami, Victoria and Moharram Bey. **Targeting them, or choosing a "juice shop" category, puts the factory in front of people who want a glass of mango and drives them away angry** |
| عصير طبيعي · عصير اورجانيك · صحي للأطفال | **Claims the product cannot back up.** The never-say list in [[Brand Voice Guide]] and [[Alex Foods Discovery Brief]] §A2. TSA runs the account |
| Anything with a price in it (بكام · سعر) | **Prices are not being collected** ([[Alex Foods Discovery Brief]] §3). The standing answer is that the price varies by shop |

## 5. The competitor read — the digital shelf

**The shelf walk was dropped ([[Decisions]] #29), so this is the version that can be done without a shop visit.** Below are the names that showed up in search results for the same categories, each with where it showed up. **None of them has been read properly yet.** That needs Facebook and the delivery apps, and both are blocked here.

| Range it competes with | Name | Where it showed up | What it tells us |
|---|---|---|---|
| **BeBo** (kids' drink) | **Beyti Bee**, 225 ml | Carrefour Egypt, in a dedicated **kids-juice** category | Big retailers have a kids'-drink shelf online, **and nobody from Alex Foods is on it** |
| BeBo | **Mirolland** mango, pouches in a carton of 10 | babelgomla.com, a wholesale-to-shops site | **A pouch competitor sold to shops by the carton online** |
| BeBo | «عصير بست ظرف», carton of 40 | gomlaexpress.com, wholesale | Same format, same buyer |
| BeBo · AlRawy | **Juhayna · Faragello · Beyti** | Search results and retailer pages | The national juice brands. **Faragello's own video shows a factory in New Borg El Arab, inside Alexandria governorate**, which makes it a local competitor for trade searches too |
| **2MAN · POLEKA** (frozen) | **Mega** (Nestlé) ice pops · **Jersey** (Ice Food) · **Popsicle / Bopsel** · **Sila** jelly ice lollies | Their own pages and Facebook posts in search results | **Sila's post targets wholesale traders with discounts**, the same trader audience cluster B is after |

### What search surfaced that nobody asked about: wholesale sites

**Shop owners buy cartons on B2B apps and sites:** gomlamarket, babelgomla, suqplus, gomlaexpress. **Competing pouches are listed there. Search showed no Alex Foods product on any of them.** **For "more shops", that is probably a bigger lever than anything on Google Maps.**

> **It is not in the scope.** Listing on wholesale platforms is distribution work, and the client runs its own distributors, who may not want a platform in the middle. **So it goes in as a recommendation in the delivery message and a clause 7 conversation, never as work done for free.** It belongs alongside the trade-material upsell in [[Alex Foods Discovery Brief]] §13.

### What the competitor read still needs, and from whom

**Aly, from the iPad, about 30 minutes:**
1. **Meta Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library, country Egypt): search each name in the table, and note who is running ads right now, the format and the offer. **Blocked from here.**
2. **One delivery app** (Talabat or Breadfast): prices and pack sizes for Beyti Bee, Mirolland and whichever ice pops are listed, next to BeBo if it is there. **Blocked from here.**
3. **Google on the phone, in Alexandria:** type the first half of «عصير بيبو», «مصنع عصير اسكندرية» and «ايس بوب جملة» and screenshot what autocomplete suggests. **Free, and the closest thing to real demand data until Keyword Planner is available.**

**What this route can never give, said plainly in the book:** where each pack sits on a real shelf, and what is in the box by the till. That was the shelf walk, and it was dropped.

## 6. The monthly routine — what Package A's maintenance line pays for

**The contract says:** *"update listing data, posts and photos; track local search visibility in Alexandria and surroundings."* It sets no quantity, so this is TSA's proposal, **sized so it costs almost nothing on top of the month's production:**

| Every month | What | Cost to TSA |
|---|---|---|
| **2 Google profile posts** | One to consumers, **one to traders**, both reusing that month's graphics from [[Month 1 Asset Brief]] | Near zero: resized versions of assets already made |
| **Photos** | The month's best 3 to 5 product visuals, plus any real factory or truck photo the client sends | Near zero |
| **Business data check** | The §3 master against the Google profile, Facebook About, Instagram and TikTok. **Fix any difference the same day** | 10 minutes |
| **Visibility read** | From the Google profile's own Performance screen: searches, the words people used, calls, direction requests, website clicks. **Plus the 10 tracked searches from §4, checked from an Alexandria location** | 20 minutes |
| **Reviews** | Reply to every review within 48 hours in the brand voice, under [[Brand Voice Guide]] §8. **Complaints escalate to Mohamed the same day** | As they come |

**The monthly report carries one line from this:** calls and direction requests from the profile, **and any trader or distributor enquiry it produced.** That line is what ties the engagement KPI to the "more shops" goal ([[Alex Foods Discovery Brief]] §A3).

## How we got here

- **Planned as a shelf walk plus desk research** in [[Foundation Roadmap]] Phase 2: two Alexandria shop visits with a phone camera for prices, pack sizes and shelf position. **Dropped 20 September ([[Decisions]] #29)**, and the competitor read became a digital-shelf read.
- **Built 23 September** from web search, after the network policy turned out to block every source that could actually be opened: Facebook, Google Maps and Trends, the directories and the delivery apps. **Hence every §2 finding is a lead, §4 has no volumes and §5 has an iPad step.** The footprint audit turned up four things the vault did not know: a company website, a second Facebook address, directory listings with a different address, and a WhatsApp number that disagrees with the pack by one digit.
