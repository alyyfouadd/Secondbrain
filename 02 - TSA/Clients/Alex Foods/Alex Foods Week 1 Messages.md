### Why this shape, and the rule underneath it

**The thing that makes an early payment ask smell is a bare ask with no event attached, on a date you set yourself.** TSA chose the 26th; clause 11's four-day term would have landed on the 22nd, and [[Decisions]] #2 granted the longer window as a concession. **Pulling it back to the 22nd is withdrawing a concession, and a client who paid 42,000 without being chased will notice.** That is the suspicious version.

**The Saturday is the whole trick, and it is simply true.** It makes "earlier in the week" the obvious reading without a single word asking for a date.

Four things the draft does deliberately:
