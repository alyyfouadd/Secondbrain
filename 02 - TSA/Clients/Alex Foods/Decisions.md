# Decisions — Alex Foods

**Every decision taken on this engagement, dated, with what it rests on and what it overturned.** One row per decision, newest first.

> **Why this note exists.** Until 20 September the reasoning behind every call on this client lived inline, as blockquotes correcting the paragraph above them — which is how [[Brand Voice Guide]] §2 ended up stating the old model in its body and contradicting itself underneath, and how six notes went on calling vector logos a blocker two days after that closed. **Decisions come out of the bodies and live here. The notes describe what is true now; this note describes how it got that way.**
>
> **It is also clause 4 armour.** The rejection rule protects TSA only where the approved guide matches what the client actually said, and a verbal answer that reshapes four deliverables needs a dated record with its evidence attached.

**How to use it:** before changing anything that contradicts a row below, read the row. If the decision still holds, the change is wrong. If it does not, add a new row that supersedes it — **never edit a row to make history tidier.**
