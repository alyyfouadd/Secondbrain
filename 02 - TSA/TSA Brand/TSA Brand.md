# TSA Brand

[[TSA]]'s own brand identity: the locked system, its source documents, the built kits, and the logo artwork. Not a client's brand — this is the agency's, and it governs every deliverable TSA hands over.

> **The system is in `TSA Brand System.md`; the *look* is in `TSA Complete Brand Kit.pdf`.** The note tells you the rules. The kit shows you what they produce: dark ground, corner frame marks, a grid overlay under 5%, red on one word, eyebrow labels trailing a rule, card grids. A rule read without the artifact gets rebuilt as something flatter than intended.

## Notes in this folder
- [[TSA Brand System]] — the whole system in one note: three colours with their working ratio, two typefaces, the tagline, the four logo lockups, the drift check, and **how it applies when a client's brand appears inside a TSA document**.

## Files in this folder
- `TSA Brand Lock Reference v3.1.pdf` — the one-page source. "One page. No deviations. Hand this to anyone touching the brand."
- `TSA Usage Rules.pdf` — the two-page companion: where each part of the system goes and, more importantly, where it does not.
- `tsa-lockups.png` — the logo lockups as supplied: primary, sign-off with the red period, compact, profile mark in the red circle, and the full "THE STANDARD AGENCY" horizontal.
- `TSA Complete Brand Kit.pdf` — **six pages, and the system as actually built rather than as specified.** Identity and colour, the positioning panels and brand pillars, the social kit, the do-and-don't, the content mix and posting rhythm, and the photo/footage templates. **This is the visual reference for what a TSA-made kit looks like**, and the Alex Foods kit was built from its layout. Added 18 Sep 2026.
- `TSA Social Kit.pdf` — pages 3 and 4 of the complete kit, extracted as a standalone. **Same file, not a second system** — profile setup, exact pixel sizes, post templates, rules, content mix and posting rhythm.

## What still belongs here
- **Vector logo files.** `tsa-lockups.png` is a raster reference, not artwork. The same rule TSA applies to a client applies to TSA: a logo placed from a PNG is a logo that will eventually be rebuilt badly.
- Templates as they get built: invoice, proposal, deck, social frames.
