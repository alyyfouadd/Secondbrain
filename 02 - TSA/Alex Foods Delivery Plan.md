# Alex Foods — Delivery Plan

**Dated plan to Foundation delivery.** Written 20 September 2026. **The vault has had a build order since 18 September and no dates on it**, which is how a three-week job becomes a four-week job without anybody deciding it should.

> **Everything here is anchored to two contractual dates that already exist:** the second Foundation instalment falls **8 October**, and the Foundation was targeted at **3 weeks minimum from 18 September**, which lands **9 October**. **Delivery must come before the instalment, not after it.**

## What can still break this

| Risk | State |
|---|---|
| **Seasonality unanswered** | **The biggest one.** Half the portfolio is frozen and the term runs into an Egyptian winter. It blocks §17 and it changes what the client should be paying for. |
| **The Facebook asset is a personal profile** | **Invalidates the platform strategy, not just a deliverable.** No paid campaign can run from a profile. |
| **No named approver** | Clause 3 has no top rung. **Delivery has nobody to approve it**, and clause 4's protection has nothing to stand on. |
| **BeBo photo** | Blocks BeBo copy, which is the priority range. |
| **Ad budget unnamed** | Package A includes campaign management and nobody has said what the spend is. |

> **Four of the five are one message away.** That is why the client ask is dated day one rather than "this week".
