## 1. The job in one line

**Lay out the Alex Foods brand identity and guidelines as a designed document.** TSA writes every word and supplies every value and every asset. **Mahmoud designs the pages.** He is not authoring content, not choosing colours and not naming typefaces — all three are already governed and are below.

**Why it is being commissioned at all, stated honestly for the record:** TSA's generator produces a competent text document and a mediocre identity document, [[Brand Book Spec]] §6b diagnosed exactly why, and the eight fixes it prescribed were applied in full and did not clear the bar. **This is the half of the Foundation where the design IS the content.**

## 3. The rules that cannot be broken

**These are not preferences. Each one is load-bearing and breaking it makes the document wrong rather than different.**

1. **No brand name in this system is ever set in a font.** All four wordmarks and the master seal are **artwork, placed as supplied.** Never retyped, never rebuilt from swatches, never traced — however close a face looks. [[Type System]] §1.
2. **The flavour colour owns the field. The range colour owns the logo. The master owns the seal and takes neither.** A BeBo mango pack is a mango field carrying the standard green banner; the banner does not turn orange to match the flavour, and it does not turn navy to match Alex. **This is the most-broken rule in food design and it governs the whole document.**
3. **Arabic is set first, English is fitted to it.** A layout designed in English and filled with Arabic breaks every time.
4. **Arabic leading is 1.7, Latin is 1.5.** Setting both at the same leading is the single most reliable tell of a layout built by someone who does not read Arabic.
5. **Western numerals throughout** — 0123456789, never ٠١٢٣٤٥٦٧٨٩. These layouts carry both scripts at once and mixing numeral systems reads as a mistake.
6. **Two type weights maximum in any one layout.** The packs are already loud. A third weight is how a tidy system starts looking accidental.
7. **Never enlarge a pack or a mark past its source resolution.**
8. **No stock photography anywhere.** The brands' own artwork only.
9. **Page numbers are real and quotable.** Someone has to be able to say "page 34" on a phone call.
10. **The seal goes on white or Paper and on nothing else.** On navy its ring reads 1.43:1 and disappears.

### And the eight things that made the last version read cheap

**Handed over as a reject list rather than as advice, because they are the specific failures this commission exists to fix.** Full diagnosis in [[Brand Book Spec]] §6b.

Bordered boxes doing the grouping that whitespace should do · status chips styled as project-tracker pills · a type scale with only two levels so nothing leads the eye · inconsistent leftover space at the foot of inner pages · red used as a recurring full-bleed slab instead of one earned statement per section · every section opener treated identically so hierarchy is invisible · a measure long past 60–75 characters · range marks set tiny and pixelated on grey boxes.

### Who owns what

Full per-range tables in [[Colour System]] §5. The headlines Mahmoud needs on the range spreads:

- **BeBo** — Brand Green banner *(never recoloured)*, BeBo Navy wordmark keyline, five flavour fields.
- **AlRawy** — AlRawy Navy wordmark, System Red on the "y" only, Leaf Green ribbon, five flavour fields.
- **2MAN** — Ice Blue is its whole world; four colourways.
- **POLEKA** — four SKUs on three fields, each with an animal character. **Its extended social-only layer has no chosen values yet — see §7.**

> **The finding that shapes the 2MAN and POLEKA spreads: they share four values exactly, at ΔE 0.00.** They were drawn off one palette. The packs are printed and repainting is out of scope, so the system formalises it as a shared **Kids Brights** set — Ice Blue, Bright Yellow, Bright Pink, Leaf Green — that **neither brand owns.** Separation between these two is carried by **character, layout and type, never by colour.** Those two spreads have to make that visible.

## 5. Type

| Role | Face | Weights |
|---|---|---|
| Arabic and Latin text | **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** | 400, 600, 700 |
| Latin set alone | IBM Plex Sans | 400, 600, 700 |
| Codes and specifications | IBM Plex Mono | 400, 600 |

All three are free under the SIL Open Font License. **One bilingual superfamily, so there is no visible seam where a line switches script.** Mono is not decoration: HEX values in a proportional face do not align in a column, and an `8` reads as a `B` at 9pt.

**Document scale** — cover 72pt / 1.05 · section opener 48pt / 1.1 · page heading 28pt / 1.2 · sub-head 16pt / 1.3 · body 10.5pt / 1.5 Latin and 1.7 Arabic · caption 8.5pt / 1.45 · code 9pt / 1.4.

**The 28pt and 16pt levels are specified and were not being used in the old version** — that is cheap-tell #4. **Use them.**

**On-canvas minimum for any social asset shown in the guidelines: nothing below 22px.** Below that it is unreadable at phone thumbnail size.

## 7. Open — three decisions Aly owes before the identity sections are final

**All three are in [[Active Priorities]] §1 and none is Mahmoud's to make.**

1. **Which master seal governs: with the Egyptian flag ribbon, or without?** `logo-alex-seal.jpg` has it; the vector does not. **Two versions of the master mark is the thing a Foundation exists to stop**, and §06 cannot be laid out without the answer.
2. **Does the master keep its own blue, or share 2MAN's?** The vector's authored ring blue collapses into 2MAN blue at ΔE2000 3.77 under the system's own rule. Same object as question 1.
3. **POLEKA's extended colours.** Four SKUs across three fields, two of them the brand's own colour. **The extended layer is mandatory there and has no values behind it**, so the POLEKA spread has a hole in it.

*Cover ground — navy with the seal, or type-only on Paper — is a fourth open item, but it is Mahmoud's call to propose now that the cover is his.*
