## The gating chain — read this before planning any work

Four gates sit in front of delivery, and they fire in order. Three of them are outside my control, which is the whole risk on this job.

1. **Signed acceptance page.** Work does not begin until the client returns page 3 signed, naming one approver with role and contact.
2. **Client materials, all seven.** No delivery starts before these arrive (list below).
3. **Foundation clock: 3 weeks minimum**, starting from **the first instalment (10,000 on 22 Sep 2026) OR the arrival of the complete materials list — whichever is later.** Materials arriving late push the start; the money alone does not start it.
4. **Package A starts only after Brand Foundation is signed off in writing — not from the payment date.** Month 1 counts from the written approval date.

**The consequence worth holding onto:** Package A's 42,000 is marked received, but its clock has not started and cannot start until Foundation is approved. Money in hand is not the same as a month running.

And clause 6: if the client is late with materials, product, or approvals, the **timeline extends day for day — but the paid month does not extend.**

## Stage 2 — Package A (42,000 EGP/month × 3)

The monthly retainer that executes what the Foundation defined. These quantities repeat **every month** for the term.

- [ ] **2 × Product Animations** — premium short product videos, built on the approved shooting recipe, delivered ready to publish.
- [ ] **6 × Animated Stills** — static product shots with simple motion. Lighter and faster than full animation; works for feed and story.
- [ ] **12 × Graphics** — feed and story, split between the two, in approved colours and fonts, copy written in the brand voice.
- [ ] **1 × Content Calendar** — same format as the Foundation's, for the new month, delivered before that month starts.
- [ ] **GBP + Local SEO maintenance** — update listing data, posts and photos; track local search visibility in Alexandria and surroundings.
- [ ] **Paid Campaign Management** — campaign build, audience targeting, performance monitoring and in-flight optimisation. **Media spend does not pass through TSA** — the client pays the platforms directly.
- [ ] **1 × Giveaway slogan and campaign lockup** — the campaign slogan and the fixed visual treatment carried across every giveaway asset.

### How a month runs
1. **Calendar** — arrives before the month begins.
2. **Production** — execution against the approved calendar.
3. **Revision** — one revision round per delivery, collected into one written reply.
4. **Approval** — no reply within **48 hours** and the delivery counts as approved.

### Not included — and this is the delivery risk
**No shoot is included**, in either stage. TSA hands over the shooting recipe *in writing*; the client supplies product samples or ready photographs. Any shoot TSA actually executes is quoted separately under clause 7.

So the entire monthly output — 2 animations, 6 animated stills, 12 graphics — depends on the client providing usable product imagery. If what arrives is unusable, production stalls on a month that is already paid for and does not extend.

## Terms and conditions — 11 clauses, applying to both stages

1. **Creative direction.** TSA owns execution. The client approves or rejects a delivery as a whole, and does not specify shot angle, colour, font, music, pacing or animation style. Notes are written as a required *outcome*, not as execution instructions.
2. **Revisions.** One round per delivery, included in the price, all notes gathered into a single written reply. Extra rounds are charged separately.
3. **Approval.** One named approver on the client side; only their notes count. No reply in 48 hours means approved.
4. **Rejection.** Must state a written reason. A delivery matching the agreed brief and the approved foundation guide counts as delivered.
5. **Files.** Final deliverables are licensed to the client **on full payment**. Raw files, project files and TSA's working method stay agency property.
6. **Client delay.** Day for day. Late materials, product or approvals extend the schedule — but not the paid month.
7. **Scope.** Anything not written in the document is quoted and agreed in writing before it starts.
8. **Portfolio rights.** TSA may show the work in its portfolio, on its own pages and in client pitches, once it has gone public.
9. **Renewal and exit.** 3-month term, no auto-renewal; renewal requires written agreement from both sides. Either party exiting early gives 20 days notice, and the paid month runs to its end.
10. **Governing law.** Arab Republic of Egypt.
11. **Payment.** 50% minimum upfront before work starts, due within 4 days of invoice, 2% per week late penalty, via InstaPay or Vodafone Cash.
